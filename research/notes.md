## The building

- 55 Baring Street, N1 3DS, on a corner of a residential crescent between Islington and Hoxton,
  just off New North Road. A pub here by 1856 (pubology). Earlier names: Baring Arms, the Poet (2019).
- Locally listed (CAMRA North London): 1840s pub front, three storeys, yellow stock brick, five bays,
  quoins and a parapet; central first-floor window with a triangular pediment; rendered ground floor with
  coupled Corinthian pilasters at the corners and a central door framed by pilasters; big glazed
  ground-floor windows. Repaired after wartime bomb damage.
- The 2019 photo shows the front gently bowed, white stucco quoins and window surrounds.
- CAMRA (Dec 2025): the pub was listed for sale at £1.5m. Not on the site; worth knowing before the pitch.

## The room

- Ground floor (Resy 2022): "a skewed rectangle of room; all greige panelling, bare off-white walls,
  forest green banquettes, and retro-utilitarian metal shades". About 40 covers then.
- The Nudge 2022: "linen-coloured walls, forest green panelling and a cluster of refurbed, second-hand
  furniture in warm, polished woods". Infatuation: "shiny, polished brown wood and exposed brick walls
  painted white"; candles lit and lights dimmed in the evening. LOTI: "dark green paint and dried flowers".
- Bar: "prominent bar lined with elegant taps", "timeless signage" (Resy). About eight stools at the bar
  for a pint (Hot Dinners 2022). Braise (2024) eats lunch perched at the bar.
- First floor: private dining room for up to 32, next to the open kitchen, full-height windows, "bathed in
  natural light" by day, "lights are dimmed, and an abundance of candles lit" in the evening, air
  conditioned (their 2024 brochure). The kitchen is upstairs (Hot Dinners).
- Critics on the mood: Michelin "cool simplicity with the décor"; Guardian "Stylish simplicity in the
  decor"; Standard, less kind: "austere, somewhat formal room". Harden's: "calming former pub".

## The food

- Short printed menu, no section headings, dishes written "X, Y & Z", prices as bare numbers on the
  à la carte, £ only on the feasting menu. Set in STIX General, black on white. Pre-dinner drinks at the
  top, dessert wines and digestifs at the bottom. Notes: "Please speak to a member of staff for allergen
  information prior to ordering" and, on the à la carte only, "Game may contain shot".
- Tecwyn (Resy): "three things on a plate and very little fluff". Influences: Turkish, North African,
  Spanish, Italian, Japanese touches through a modern European lens. "We're just a pub."
- Constants across four years of reviews: quail shish, garlic yoghurt & pul biber chilli (still on the
  10 Oct menu at £14); triple-cooked chips with garlic mayonnaise; grilled hispi cabbage; the sirloin or
  rib for two; Sunday roasts with Yorkshire pudding.
- Sourcing (Resy, Aug 2022): fruit and veg from Primeur; fish from Flying Fish, Cornwall; meat from the
  Rhug Estate, north Wales (organic, regenerative). Hot Dinners 2022 adds Wildfarmed flour for bread, no
  single-use plastics, fryer oil to biofuel. GFG later mentions Rhug Estate venison. The current menu lists
  Highland beef, Middle White and Swaledale pork, Black Prestige chicken, Cornish skate, so the meat
  suppliers may have moved on. The site says "when we opened" for these. **Confirm current suppliers.**
- Staff: "a commitment to a 48-hour work week for staff" (Resy 2022). Framed on the site as how they
  opened. **Confirm it still holds.**

## Lunch deal, specials, events

- Announced 2022: Tue–Fri, a weekly-changing dish with a glass of wine, £15 (Restaurant Online, Staff
  Canteen). Standard Aug 2022: £12, or £15 with a glass of house wine. Braise, Oct 2024: still £12/£15,
  written on "a small blackboard near the door"; occasional steak frites weeks.
- **Not confirmed for 2026.** Nothing on the current site, no lunch PDF, Instagram not reachable.
  Left off the site and listed as an open question.
- Summer Sunday BBQ: documented in Aug 2023 (The Resident, "until August 27") and Aug 2024 (The London
  100). No 2026 evidence. Left off.
- Sister pub: the Kerfield Arms, Camberwell, opened early 2025 by the same pair (Harden's; an Instagram
  caption surfaced in search). One line on the story page.

## How they talk about themselves

- Their own site: almost nothing. Lower case, plain, functional: "please email enquiries@thebaring.co.uk
  for further information and availability".
- Brochure: warm but plain. "Come the evening the lights are dimmed, and an abundance of candles lit to
  create a warm and atmospheric space." "Driven by quality sourcing and simple ingredient led cooking our
  feasting menu is a convivial and celebratory affair."
- In interviews: Symonds wants "good ingredients cooked well, on a plate"; "It's been a boozer, on and
  off, for 200 years." Tecwyn: "I really don't want it to be about me", "We're just a pub." Resy noted
  the opening came "with relatively little fanfare", probably on purpose.
- Instagram @thebaring: couldn't load it (login wall, rate-limited). Search snippets show ~19K followers,
  ~122 posts, and a recent caption pushing private dining for up to 32 and the Kerfield Arms.
- Voice for the site: short declaratives, no adjectives they wouldn't print on a menu, British spelling,
  no exclamation marks, times written the way their site writes them (12:00, 14.30).

## Who comes when

- **Weekday lunch:** quiet. "An oasis of calm", "there always seems to be a table free"; solo diners,
  new parents, couples; people eating at the bar (Braise 2024). The lunch special was built for this.
- **Early evening:** families. Infatuation: around 6pm "as many little Islingtonites… as there are big ones".
- **Dinner:** busy. "Dinner bookings disappear fast" (Braise). Friday night, walk-ins turned away and
  "perching at the end of the bar with a pint… didn't seem like an option" (Gourmet Gorro, Feb 2025).
- **Sunday:** roasts until 16.00, close at 18:00. "Superb Sunday lunch" (Harden's diners). Summer BBQs in
  past years.
- **Drinkers:** bar stools, cask (Five Points), Lost & Grounded house lager, Belgian bottles, Tilquin to
  share. CAMRA saw "some punters were just having a session". Dog friendly (Harden's, CAMRA).
- **Groups:** first-floor room to 32, £60 feasting menu, linen hire, pre-order by email.

## What reviewers praise and complain about

Praise: the cooking (quail shish, chips, hispi, roasts), imagination without fuss, warm service, the
beer, the wine, value of the lunch special, candles in the evening, "no-frills good service", dogs welcome.

Complaints: hard to walk in at dinner; room can read formal or austere (Standard); "£7 pint country"
(Standard); lunch special light, service charge pushes it towards £20 (Braise); an occasional main that
"clang[s] the same repeated note" (Standard); sauces strong in big doses (Gorro). Harden's scores
service a notch below food and ambience.

## Design notes

- **Logo:** their brochure mark, used as-is (vectors), never redrawn. THE / BARING in the header; the
  full mark with ✦ and N1 on the story page and og.jpg. The four-point star is their ornament (brochure
  dividers and covers) and becomes the site's only icon: dividers, list markers, and the kitchen-status light.
- **Type:** STIX Two Text, the successor to STIX General, which is what the printed menus are set in.
  One family. Labels in letter-spaced capitals, a nod to the brochure headers and to the tracked capitals
  on the current site. Prices in tabular figures, right-aligned, no £, like the card.
- **Colour:** paper = the linen walls (#F1ECE2); ink = the menu's black, warmed (#1D1B17); green = the
  grey-green panelling and banquettes (#2F3B33, lit #5E6B60), used for actions only; greige rules
  (#CFC6B4). Evening palette from the candlelit photos: dark #15110D, candlelit linen #EFE4D0, flame #E2A85A.
- **Layout:** set like their menu card. A narrow column of text on paper, thin rules with star ends, photos
  framed with a margin (as the brochure frames them), never full-bleed. Desktop adds a fixed left column
  (logo, kitchen clock, book) beside the scrolling content.
- **Signature 1, kitchen clock:** the first thing on every page is whether the kitchen is serving,
  in their food windows ("Lunch until 14.30", "Kitchen back at 18:00, the bar's open", "Roasts until 16.00",
  "Closed today, back Tuesday"). The menu opens on à la carte or Sunday to match. A week strip shows the
  food windows as bars and can be dragged to see any other time.
- **Signature 2, candlelight:** from 18:00 London time the site dims, as the room does: dark paper,
  candlelit text, the star flickers, and the photos swap from the sunlit room to the candlelit tables.
- **Feast planner:** the private-room page builds an October feast for up to 32, with live per-head and total
  including 12.5% service, then pre-fills an enquiry (front-end confirmation only).
- **The pub front:** a line drawing of the 1840s front from the CAMRA description and the 2019 photo,
  because there's no photo of the outside or the bar. Its windows light up when the pub is open and glow
  amber after dark: the "you can just walk in" message without a photo.
